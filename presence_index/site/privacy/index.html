Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Presence is a camera monitoring app, run by ProdBytes ("we", "us") at presence.nu01.com and in the Presence apps for Android, iOS and Linux. This policy says what Presence collects, why, where it's kept, who else handles it, and what you can ask of us. It applies to you as a user of Presence. If you record other people with it, you're responsible for them too: see Recording other people.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your recordings to train models for anyone else. We don't see your card details: Apple, Google or Stripe handle payments.

Where it's kept, and for how long

  • On your devices. Recordings and events stay on the device that made them, for the history you choose in Settings (two weeks by default; one day to three months), then they're deleted.
  • In the cloud, for Premium. Clips, events, tagged frames and settings are uploaded to Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States, in a folder only your profile can reach. Uploads are deleted after 90 days.
  • Between your devices, for free accounts. Events and clip thumbnails pass between your devices through AWS IoT while they're connected; they aren't kept in the cloud.
  • Encryption. Every recording and image is encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM) before it's stored or sent. Your devices share their keys with each other through your profile's settings. Event details (times, names, tags, locations) aren't encrypted, so the service can sort and sync them.
  • Accounts, profiles and roles are kept while your account exists, and deleted with it (see Deleting your account). Feedback conversations are kept until they're deleted on request or with the account. Logs are kept for as long as we need them to run and secure the service.

Who else handles it

  • Google: sign-in (Google Identity Services).
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, storage, databases, device sync and the recognition service.
  • Apple and Google: Premium bought in the iPhone or Android app, under their privacy policies. We ask them whether a purchase is valid; they don't get your email from us.
  • Stripe: Premium subscriptions and payments, under Stripe's privacy policy. Your email goes to Stripe with the checkout.
  • rbacr, our role service at rbacr.nu01.com, which keeps who may use Presence and who is Premium, by email.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles, when a map is shown (your browser asks it for them directly).
  • We may disclose data when the law requires it, or to protect people or the service.

Recording other people

Presence records whatever its cameras see and hear. Before a device records, whoever sets it up confirms they have the right to record there, and that faces used to identify people are biometric data. Where you record, and whom, is your decision and your responsibility: you may need to tell people they're being recorded, get their consent before naming or recognising them, and delete their data when they ask. For those recordings you're the controller, and we process them on your behalf.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete events, devices and their data in the app; sign out to stop syncing.
  • Delete your account in the app, at the end of your account (Delete account): if you own your profile, it deletes the whole profile and everything in it in the cloud; if your account is linked to someone else's profile, it only unlinks it. You can also ask us by email. Deleting your account says how, and what is deleted and kept.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and everything in it, by writing to the address below. We answer within 30 days.
  • Cancel Premium at any time where you bought it (the App Store, Google Play, or Stripe); your account becomes free at the end of the paid period.
  • If you're in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection law, you can also object to processing, ask us to restrict it, and complain to your data protection authority. We rely on our contract with you to run the service, on your consent for location and recognition, and on our legitimate interest in keeping it secure.

Children

Presence isn't meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their accounts.

Changes

We'll post changes here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app before a change that affects how we use your data takes effect.
