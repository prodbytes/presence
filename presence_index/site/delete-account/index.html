Delete your account

Effective 14 October 2026

You can delete your Presence account at any time, in the app or by writing to us. Presence is run by ProdBytes. This page says how, and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open Presence and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your account: the Profile tab, or your picture at the top right.
  3. At the end, tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, tick I understand that this can't be undone, and tap Delete account.

It works the same on Android, iOS, Linux and the web. Your account is deleted at once, the device you used forgets the profile's events and clips, and you're signed out.

Without the app

Write to julio@nu01.com from the email address of the account you want deleted (the Google account you sign in with), with the subject "Delete my Presence account". We may reply to that address to confirm it's you before deleting anything. We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and tell you when it's done.

What is deleted

Your data belongs to a profile. The account that made the profile is its owner; other accounts can be linked to it.

  • If you own the profile, the whole profile goes:
    • its clips, recordings, thumbnails, tagged frames, events and device settings in the cloud;
    • its Feedback & Help conversation;
    • the faces and pets it taught the cloud recognition service (labels and the model);
    • its list of devices, its Raspberry Pi stream keys and any unused link codes;
    • its cloud identity, so no device can sync with it any more;
    • the profile itself, your account's link to it, and every other account's link to it: those accounts lose access to it.
  • If your account is linked to someone else's profile, only your link goes, with your own Feedback & Help messages. The profile and its data stay with its owner.
  • On the device you delete from, the profile's events, clips and recordings, and the other devices' encryption keys, are deleted, and you're signed out.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Cloud backups. Deleted cloud files stay as previous versions for up to 30 days, and database backups for up to 35 days; then they're gone. Nobody uses them except to recover from a fault in the service.
  • Your other devices. A device still signed in to the profile keeps its own copies until its History setting deletes them (two weeks by default, three months at most), or until you delete the app there. Events a device recorded while nobody was signed in stay on that device.
  • Your access and subscription. Whether your email may use Presence, and whether it's Premium, are kept by our role service (rbacr) by email, so a subscription keeps working if you come back. Ask us by email to delete that too.
  • Payments. Apple, Google or Stripe keep their records of your payments as the law requires (see Stripe's privacy policy). We keep which App Store or Google Play subscription belonged to your account (its ID, product and end date, with your email), so it can't be claimed by another account.
  • Logs. Technical logs, kept as long as we need them to run and secure the service (see the Privacy Policy).

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it first, where you bought it:

  • In the iPhone app (the App Store): in your Apple account's subscriptions, or Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • In the Android app (Google Play): in Google Play's subscriptions.
  • On the web (Stripe): every receipt email from Stripe has a link to manage or cancel the subscription. If you can't find one, write to julio@nu01.com and we'll cancel it for you.
